## Design Context

### Users
Current and potential members of a local tabletop gaming association in Portugalete, Bizkaia (Basque Country, Spain). Mix of experienced gamers and newcomers curious about RPGs, wargames, and board games. They visit the site to learn about the association, find upcoming events (themed nights like May the 4th, Halloween), download the companion app, or get directions to the venue.

### Brand Personality
**Welcoming, ambitious, spirited.** La Orden is a community guild — friendly enough to walk into as a complete beginner, serious enough to run organized campaigns and host themed events. Not a corporate gaming platform. Not a generic dark-mode fantasy template. It should feel like the best local game shop you've ever walked into.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Anti-reference**: Generic AI dark-mode with gold accents, glowing borders, gradient text, hero metric layouts. The current site falls into this pattern and needs to break out of it.
- **Mood**: Warm community space with character. Think: the notice board at your favorite game shop, old board game box art, a well-worn tavern table where stories happen.
- **Theme**: Warm — not cold dark mode. Rich, earthy tones that feel lived-in and inviting.
- **Key constraint**: No framework. Simple static HTML/CSS/JS. Events must be trivially easy to add/edit.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth over spectacle** — Every element should feel inviting, not intimidating. Approachable first, impressive second.
2. **Character over polish** — Prefer distinctive, slightly imperfect choices over generic smoothness. A site with personality beats a site with production value.
3. **Community over content** — The site exists to get people through the door and to events. Every section should serve that goal.
4. **Simple to maintain** — A non-technical person should be able to add an event by editing a clearly-marked section of HTML or a simple data structure.
